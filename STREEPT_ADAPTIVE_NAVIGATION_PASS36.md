# Streept Pass 36 — Adaptive Navigation

Streept now uses its local observation ledger to adapt driver guidance on roads with repeated evidence of difficult maneuvers.

- Road difficulty score 0–100 from completed/missed maneuvers, lane misalignment and hazards.
- Sparse evidence has deliberately low confidence.
- Learned difficulty can extend preparation distance and escalate very difficult roads.
- Hard safety/restriction/hazard decisions remain authoritative.
- No raw GPS traces or paid services are introduced.
